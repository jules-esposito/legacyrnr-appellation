Partnership Kickoff

LegacyRnR is a luxury vacation rental management and investment company in Sonoma County. Its homes are built for gatherings of family and friends, with high-touch service from a local team. Guests who book select LegacyRnR homes receive perks at Appellation Healdsburg for the duration of their stay.

Guest Perks
  • Stays of 3 nights or more at select LegacyRnR homes: a custom, co-branded food amenity (wine chips) and a bottle of Charlie Palmer's Aureole Cabernet.
  • Every stay, any length: 2 keys to the property, which grant access to the perks below.

Resort keys

Pools, Terroir spa, fitness center and lounge for the whole stay. Keys stay deactivated until check-in at the front desk and deactivate again at checkout. We are not limiting the number of guests at this time.

Priority tables

Priority reservations at Folia Bar & Kitchen and Andys Beeline Rooftop.

The first glass

A welcome beverage at Folia or Andys Beeline, with compliments from LegacyRnR.

10% off spa and cabanas

10% off Terroir spa treatments and pool cabanas for the length of the stay.

How a stay moves between the two teams
Legacy

At booking

Sends an arrival notice and creates a shared calendar entry for every program reservation, including owner stays and guests with no requests. Each event shows guest name, party size and phone number. Guest emails are shared so they can be added to the Appellation database.

Legacy

Guest outreach

Welcome message at booking, then reminders ahead of the stay. Guests submit dining, spa and pool requests through a short form (Airbnb guests reply in the thread).

Legacy

Request email

One consolidated email per stay from the dedicated LegacyRnR program inbox, using the template below. Subject line: [House Name] / [Family Last Name].

Appellation

Confirm by reply

The concierge replies to the same email with what is booked, what is waitlisted and what could not be accommodated. Legacy updates the guest.

Appellation

Arrival and checkout

Front desk sees the shared calendar. Guest checks in with the home key, which activates until checkout. Reservations are flagged in OpenTable for the host stand.

At the front desk: when the guest arrives to activate their key, the front desk liaison sends an arrival confirmation email to LegacyRnR using the arrival confirmation template below, confirming how many people are in the party.

What is the latest that Appellation can receive reservations from the LegacyRnR team?

Reservation requests are due 2 weeks before the start of the stay. Anything received after that is handled on a best-effort basis.

Who is responsible for the F&B amenity inventory?

Inventory of Aureole Cabernet and wine chips is solely the responsibility of the LegacyRnR team.

Who is responsible for key inventory?

The LegacyRnR team tracks key inventory. LegacyRnR must give the Appellation Healdsburg team 2 weeks' notice when new inventory is needed.

What if a guest arrives without reservations?

Their name will be on the shared calendar. We verify their identity, then book them on a best-effort basis, subject to availability. They still have full access to the pool and gym.

What is waiting in the home for every stay?

A handwritten-style welcome card, the partnership pamphlet and the resort keys.

How do we quantify how many guests are using the perks?

The front desk liaison confirms pool and gym use at check-in. We will review the numbers over the 30-day trial, then adjust.
